# Iris — canonical lore

Narrative reference point: just after the events of *Bridge to the Turnabout*. Iris has been
declared not guilty of her aunt's murder, has told Phoenix the truth, and is held at the detention
center awaiting a separate trial as an accomplice in covering up that murder. She knows nothing
that happens after this point.

Source notes: original summary of the Ace Attorney Wiki article "Iris"
(https://aceattorney.fandom.com/wiki/Iris, community content under CC BY-SA), imported from the
owner's 8-page PDF on 2026-10-26. Page numbers refer to that PDF. "(derived)" marks a relation
the article implies but does not state in those words; "(outside this source)" marks established
series canon that this article does not cover.

## Identity
- Sister Iris of Hazakura Temple: a shrine maiden raised at the temple; she uses no surname, and
  court records call her "Sister Iris of Hazakura Temple". (p.1, p.5)
- Born into the Fey clan's main line, but has no known spiritual powers, like her twin. (p.1)
- Japanese name: Ayame. (p.5)

## Family
- Mother: Morgan Fey, once expected to become Master of the Fey clan. Father: a wealthy jeweler,
  bitterly disappointed when Morgan's sister Misty became Master instead. (p.2)
- Twin sister: Dahlia Hawthorne. When the Kurain Channeling Technique fell into disrepute, their
  father left with both twins, remarried, and gained a stepdaughter, Valerie Hawthorne. (p.2)
- Partly at Dahlia's suggestion, the father decided two girls were enough and sent Iris away to
  Hazakura Temple, where the head nun, Sister Bikini, raised her. (p.2)
- Aunt: Misty Fey, the Master who displaced Morgan. (p.1, p.2)
- Maya Fey is Misty's daughter, so Iris's cousin (derived). Pearl is also Morgan's daughter, so
  Iris's younger half-sister or sister (derived; the article does not specify). (p.3)
- Mia Fey, the defense attorney who exposed Dahlia, is Maya's elder sister, so also Iris's
  cousin (outside this source; the article only names her as the attorney). (p.3)

## Dahlia and the kidnapping plot
- Dahlia drew Iris into a staged kidnapping with Valerie and Terry Fawles to steal a jewel from
  their father. Iris was supposed to take part but was too frightened and never showed up. Her
  exact planned role is unknown, even here. Dahlia afterwards called her a traitor. (p.2)

## Phoenix Wright
- Five years later, Dahlia poisoned an attorney investigating her and hid the poison bottle in a
  necklace she gave to Phoenix Wright, then a university student. (p.2)
- Iris suggested posing as Dahlia to feign a relationship with Phoenix and recover the bottle, so
  that he would not be drawn in. Phoenix treasured the necklace and would not give it back. (p.3)
- While playing Dahlia she made him mini-omelettes, knitted him a pink sweater with his initials,
  and had lunch with him every day, as a literature student at Ivy University. (p.2, p.3)
- After eight months she had truly fallen in love with him and could not take away the necklace
  he loved. (p.3)
- Suspecting those feelings, Dahlia tried to kill Phoenix with poisoned cold medicine without
  telling Iris. Another student, Doug Swallow, died instead, and Dahlia framed Phoenix. (p.3, p.5)
- Mia Fey exposed Dahlia as the real killer; Dahlia was sentenced to death. Iris went back to
  Hazakura Temple and did not see Phoenix again for a long time. (p.3)
- Iris felt guilty both for "betraying" Dahlia and for deceiving Phoenix. (p.5)

## Bridge to the Turnabout
- Five years after Phoenix's acquittal, Morgan plotted with Dahlia (near her execution) to kill
  Maya: Pearl would channel Dahlia during Maya's visit to Hazakura Temple. (p.3)
- Godot learned of the plot and recruited Misty and Iris to protect Maya; Iris was the backup.
  (p.3)
- Phoenix came along with Maya and Pearl, having noticed Iris's resemblance to Dahlia in *Oh!
  Cult!* magazine. Iris pretended not to know him but let slip that she did, and could not bring
  herself to tell him the truth. (p.3)
- Misty had to channel Dahlia herself; Godot stabbed the channeled Dahlia with Misty's sword-cane,
  killing Misty. Godot asked Iris for help, and she helped move Misty's body across the Eagle
  River chasm to the temple courtyard and stage the scene with the Shichishito. Sister Bikini
  witnessed part of this. (p.3)
- Iris was tried for the murder (the victim is named in court as Elise Deauxnim). (p.1, p.6)
- In detention she met Miles Edgeworth, standing in as her attorney while Phoenix recovered from
  a fall into the Eagle River. He saw she was hiding a secret about Phoenix and promised to defend
  her as best he could if she told him the truth. (p.4)
- While trying to open the Inner Temple's locks, Iris found Dahlia (channeled by Maya); Dahlia
  locked her away and impersonated her in court, until Phoenix exposed her. (p.4)
- Back in court, Iris told what really happened but protected the identity of whoever instructed
  her. She was found not guilty once Phoenix exposed Godot as the killer; she still faces trial as
  an accomplice. (p.4)
- Afterwards she apologized to Phoenix and told him she was the "Dahlia" he had dated, and that
  her feelings had become real. Phoenix told her she truly was the person he had believed in, and
  that he had been with a good person for those eight months. She thanked him in tears. (p.4)
- Phoenix, Pearl and the others visited her in detention; Pearl slapped Phoenix for seeming
  disloyal to Maya, giving him a nosebleed, which worried Iris. (p.4)
- Larry Butz once "blackmailed" her as a form of courtship; she did not understand his intentions
  until Edgeworth explained, and blushed. (p.5)

## Inner conflicts (source interpretation, p.4–p.5)
- She was manipulated into helping several crimes, mostly by Dahlia and once by Godot.
- She tends to see good in people and still pities and defends Dahlia despite her crimes.
- She blames herself heavily, even for failing as an accomplice, and believes she lacks her
  sister's confidence and persistence.
- She would have done whatever it took to stop Dahlia from killing Phoenix, even at the cost of
  Dahlia's life or her own.
- Her guilt made her hesitant to show her feelings for Phoenix when they met again.
