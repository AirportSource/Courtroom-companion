Japanese Reading Assistant — Dictionary Sources

Source: official full multilingual JMdict, snapshot and download 26 October 2026. This application extracts Japanese forms, restrictions, usage metadata, English glosses and verified French glosses into compressed lookup shards. No dictionary meanings are generated by an LLM. Sense candidates are not contextual translations.

The derived dictionary data (not the application code) is redistributed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0. See the EDRDG General Dictionary Licence Statement and project documentation.

French contributions

French glosses are included only when the same gloss and lexical entry ID are present in the JMdict internationalization project, by its French translation contributors, coordinated by Alexandre Courbot / Tagaini Jisho. Their licence statement releases these contributions under CC BY-SA 3.0; this adapted data is distributed under the compatible later CC BY-SA 4.0 licence. The older FJ dictionary is not independently redistributed here.

Coverage: all JMdict English entries, plus a verified subset of French glosses (counts in the manifest). Missing French uses English. Other languages are not included. No endorsement is implied.

Provenance, checksums and sizes: manifest.json. Rebuild/update instructions and the preprocessing source: scripts/build_jmdict.py and docs/JAPANESE_READING_ASSISTANT.md in the application source repository. Maintainers should refresh from upstream before releases and at least monthly for hosted builds.

Kuromoji and IPADIC have separate notices in assets/kuromoji/NOTICE.md. Ace Attorney proper-name readings come from a separate, attributed name list; they are not JMdict definitions.
